Notesketch Studio Guide

✍

Draw Sound Traces

Drag on the canvas to sculpt notes. Height controls pitch, left-to-right defines the musical loop timeline.

🎨

9 Expressive Instruments

Switch colors to compose with Keys, Pluck, Bell, Marimba, Flute, Strings, Chime, Bass, and 8-Bit leads.

🥁

Rhythm Layers & Tempo

Toggle Sub Kick, Snare & Hat kit, and Synth Arp on the transport bar. Drag the tempo slider or type exact BPM.

🎹

Musical Scales & Root Key

Select Major/Minor Pentatonic, Blues, Japanese Hirajoshi, Dorian, or Chromatic from the Scale menu. Tap the root octave to transpose.

🎥

Record Take & Studio Export

Hit "Record Take" to capture a video clip of your visual jamming. Export WAV audio loop, MIDI file, or high-res PNG artwork.
